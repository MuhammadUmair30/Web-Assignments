How We Make Our Cakes

Every cake starts with fresh eggs, real butter, and quality flour. We bake each layer slowly so it stays soft and moist inside.

Steps in the video

  1. Mix the batter and bake the sponge layers.
  2. Let the cake cool completely.
  3. Spread the cream and stack the layers.
  4. Cover with frosting and smooth the sides.
  5. Decorate with colors, toppings, and fondant figures.

Tip: Always cool the cake fully before adding frosting, or the cream will melt.
